## 隐私与数据说明

### 中文

**应用本身不收集个人信息。**

- 不连接互联网；
- 没有账号、广告、支付或云同步；
- 不接入统计、崩溃上报或推送 SDK；
- 题库、进度、设置、主题和提示词保存在本机；
- 导入导出只访问你主动选择的文件；
- 日志保留在本机，不会自动发送。

Android 系统备份已关闭。卸载应用或清除应用数据后，本地数据不会由系统自动恢复。

卸载应用或清除应用数据会删除本地进度。重要题库和进度请先导出备份。

联系邮箱：jytc4399@163.com

### English

**The app does not collect personal information.**

- No internet access by the app;
- No accounts, ads, payments, or cloud sync;
- No analytics, crash-upload, or push SDKs;
- Quiz banks, progress, settings, themes, and prompts are stored locally;
- Import and export only access files you explicitly select;
- Logs remain on the device and are not sent automatically.

Android system backup is disabled. Local data is not restored automatically after uninstalling or clearing the app.

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes local progress. Export important data before doing so.
